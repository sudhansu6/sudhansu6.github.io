Runs the right Nmap scans for you and reads the output into one clear result.

The problem

Nmap commands are long, easy to mistype, and the raw output takes time to read.

What it does

  • Builds the Nmap command for you and tunes it so scans finish faster.
  • Runs the scan stages automatically, one after another.
  • Reads the output and shows the final result: open ports, services and versions.
